
⛳️ Mini Golf VR
Welcome to Mini Golf VR — a fun and immersive virtual reality mini golf experience! Putt your way through creative, physics-driven courses while enjoying a relaxing VR environment.

📌 Features
🏌️‍♂️ Fully immersive VR experience

🎨 Multiple unique themed courses (Ice Arena, Haunted Mansion, and more)

🎯 Realistic golf ball physics

🏆 Single-player and local multiplayer modes

🎶 Chill background music and ambient soundscapes

📊 Score tracking and leaderboards

⚙️ Customizable golf balls and clubs

🎮 How to Play
Start the game in your VR headset.

Select a course from the main menu.

Use your controller to swing your virtual putter.

Try to get the ball in the hole in the fewest strokes possible.

Compete with friends or beat your personal best.
